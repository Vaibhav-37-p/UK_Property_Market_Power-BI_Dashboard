# UK Property Market Dashboard

A portfolio data visualisation project exploring UK house prices, regional growth, sales, property types, buyer groups, and financing patterns using the UK House Price Index dataset through May 2026.

## Dashboard Overview

The project is presented as a two-page dashboard:

- **Market Dashboard** — UK average house price, annual and monthly price change, completed sales, price trends, regional house prices, and annual regional growth.
- **Property & Buyers** — first-time buyer and former owner-occupier prices, cash vs mortgage purchase prices, property-type prices, and new-build vs existing-home comparisons.

## Key Insights

- UK average house price: **£271,295** in May 2026.
- Annual UK house price change: **+2.7%**.
- Monthly UK house price change: **+0.3%**.
- London had the highest average regional price at **£544,814**.
- Northern Ireland recorded the strongest annual regional growth at **+7.4%**.
- First-time buyer average price in England: **£244,288**.
- New-build average prices were **36.8% higher** than existing-home prices in the supplied snapshot.
